# Verification of flow / mindset research figures (full-text check)

Checked 2026-11-23. Status key: **VERIFIED** = confirmed against the full text; **CORRECTED** = full text gives a different figure; **UNVERIFIABLE** = full text could not be reached, so the figure is not confirmed (any support listed comes only from abstracts or other papers citing it).

**Access notes:** `pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov` and `www.frontiersin.org` full texts were readable over curl. PMC sometimes showed a reCAPTCHA page, but a retry got through. Three other hosts blocked the check:
- `pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov` always returned a reCAPTCHA "Checking your browser" page.
- `www.tandfonline.com` and `core.ac.uk` returned Cloudflare "Just a moment..." challenge pages (HTTP 403).
- WebFetch could not resolve any of the allowed hosts.

The full-text hosts these papers need were refused by the network proxy: eprints.leedsbeckett.ac.uk, ore.exeter.ac.uk, excli.de, mdpi.com and cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov (table images). So every Taylor & Francis paper (JASP, IRSEP) and every Elsevier or Sage paper could not be read in full.

## Short notes for the app / report

- **Si 2024 (item 5):** change the flow figure. 0.92 is the *performance* SMD. Flow ("fluency") is SMD 1.47 in Results (fixed effect), but the paper also reports 0.99 (p = 0.05). If you cite flow, use 1.47 with a caveat, or say "large but inconsistently reported".
- **PubMed 18272806 (item 8):** this is Achtziger et al. 2008, not Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006. Either relabel the link as the tennis/goal-shielding study, or link Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006 by DOI 10.1016/S0065-2601(06)38002-1.
- **Hase 2025 (item 12):** use 62 studies, N = 7,418. Describe the effects as "small correlations" rather than a single pooled d.
- **Bosshard & Gomez (item 6):** the public-performance advantage was only a trend (p = .064 for the difference). Publication-bias correction lowers the overall effect to d = 0.14.
- **Jackson 1992:** use n = 16. The "81%" figure rests on secondary citation; soften it to "most skaters reported flow as rare" or cite Swann et al. 2012 as the source.
- **Still unconfirmed in full text:** Harris r = 0.31 / CI; Swann "17 studies" and "72%". If they stay in the app, mark them as needing a check by someone who has access to Taylor & Francis or Elsevier.
